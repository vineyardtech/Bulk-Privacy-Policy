Bulk Screenshot Splitter Privacy Policy

Bulk Screenshot Splitter (com.vineyard.bulk.app, "we", "our", "us", or "the app"), developed and operated by Vineyard Tech, is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Bulk Screenshot Splitter accesses, processes, stores, and safeguards information when you download, install, and use the mobile application.

1. Information We Handle and How It Is Processed

  • Selected Text/Code Files & Generated Screenshot ZIP Archives (100% On-Device Processing): Bulk Screenshot Splitter allows you to select one or more text, log, or source-code files (such as .txt, .html, .css, .xml, .js, or .json) on your device, split them into line-numbered chunks, render them as JPG or PNG screenshot images, and bundle each file's images into a compressed .zip archive saved under your public Documents directory (/Documents/BulkScreenshotSplitter/{FolderPrefix}/). All file reading, line counting, image rendering, and ZIP compression runs strictly locally on your device. Vineyard Tech does not upload, view, collect, or store your selected files, file contents, file names, or generated screenshot ZIP archives on any Vineyard Tech server.
  • Local Generator Preferences: Bulk Screenshot Splitter stores your chosen output preferences—specifically lines per chunk (lines_per_chunk), font size (font_size), output image format (output_format), screenshot width (target_width), output folder prefix (folder_prefix), and JPEG quality (jpeg_quality)—locally inside Android SharedPreferences (bulk_screenshot_pref) so your configuration is remembered between sessions. These settings never leave your device.
  • Local Output Folder Maintenance: Before generating new ZIP archives, the app checks its own /Documents/BulkScreenshotSplitter/{FolderPrefix}/ output directory solely to clean up any leftover temporary chunk_report_*.txt files. No diagnostic reports or file contents are uploaded to any server, and release builds strip internal debug logging.
  • Support Inquiries (In-App Contact Form & Email): When you voluntarily submit a message through the in-app Contact Us screen (available on the Explore tab) or email us directly, the details you provide—specifically your name, email address, and message—are transmitted via HTTPS to our form processing provider (Formspree) and delivered to our support inbox solely so we can respond to your inquiry or bug report.

2. Device Permissions & File Access

  • Storage Access Framework (User-Selected Files): Bulk Screenshot Splitter uses Android's standard Storage Access Framework (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT) so you can manually choose the text or code files you want to convert. The app reads only the specific file URIs you select and does not scan or access unrelated files on your device.
  • Public Documents Output Storage (Scoped Storage & Legacy Compatibility):
    • On Android 10 and higher (API 29+), the app uses Android's scoped MediaStore API to write generated .zip archives into /Documents/BulkScreenshotSplitter/{FolderPrefix}/ without requesting broad storage permissions.
    • On Android 8.0 and Android 9 (API 26–28), the app requests READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (capped at maxSdkVersion="28") solely to save and manage your generated .zip archives inside the Documents directory.
    • Tapping Open Directory / Open Documents Directory launches your device's built-in system file browser (via DocumentsContract / Files UI) to view the output folder.
  • Network & Internet Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used strictly to load and display Google AdMob banner advertisements inside the app and to submit support messages if you choose to use the in-app Contact Us form.
  • No Account & No Sensitive Hardware Access: Bulk Screenshot Splitter does not require registration or login and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS, or precise GPS location.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Bulk Screenshot Splitter integrates the following third-party services, which may collect and process limited device, diagnostic, or support submission data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Displays banner advertisements at the bottom of the app's main screen. AdMob may automatically collect and process your Android Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID, IP address and approximate general location (country/city level), app and ad interactions (such as ad impressions, taps, and app launches), and performance/crash diagnostics.
  • Formspree (In-App Contact Form Processing): When you submit the optional Contact Us form inside Bulk Screenshot Splitter, your entered name, email address, and message are securely sent over HTTPS to Formspree (formspree.io) for delivery to Vineyard Tech's support team.

For more information on how Google and Formspree handle data, please review:
Google Privacy Policy · Google Advertising Technologies · Formspree Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention, User Controls & Deletion Requests

Because Bulk Screenshot Splitter does not create user accounts or maintain a cloud database of your files, your files and preferences stay on your own device and remain under your direct control.

How to Request Deletion of Support Correspondence

  1. Submit a Deletion Request by Email: Send an email to contact@vineyardtech.online or josymvine@gmail.com with the subject line Bulk Screenshot Splitter Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include Identifying Details: Send the request from (or mention) the email address you used when contacting us via the in-app Contact Us form or email so we can locate and permanently delete your support correspondence.

Processing Timeframe: All verified deletion requests for support messages and contact form submissions are completed within 30 days of receipt, with confirmation sent to your email.

In-App & Device-Level Controls

  • Clear Preferences & App Data: You can adjust any generator setting inside the Settings tab at any time, or clear app storage in Android Settings to wipe bulk_screenshot_pref and reset all settings to defaults.
  • Delete Generated ZIP Archives or Uninstall App: Uninstalling Bulk Screenshot Splitter immediately removes bulk_screenshot_pref and all internal app data. (Note: .zip files already saved into /Documents/BulkScreenshotSplitter/ remain in your device's public Documents storage until you delete them using your file manager.)
  • Third-Party Ad Telemetry Controls: Advertising identifiers processed by Google AdMob are governed by Google's retention policies. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Summary of Data Retained

  • Selected Files, Text Contents & Generated Screenshots: Never collected or retained by Vineyard Tech (processed 100% locally on your device).
  • Support Inquiries: Emails and Contact Us form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry (up to 90 days if inactive), or permanently deleted within 30 days upon request.

5. Security of Your Information

Bulk Screenshot Splitter performs all file reading, image rendering, and ZIP compression locally on your device. All external network communications—including Google AdMob and Contact Us form submissions—are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or files to any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

Bulk Screenshot Splitter is a general-purpose file and developer utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal contact details through our support form or email, please contact us so we can promptly delete that information.
